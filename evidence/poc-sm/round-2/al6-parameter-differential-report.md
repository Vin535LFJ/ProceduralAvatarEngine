## 3. Honest status of the measurements

- `spine-curve` is `DeclaredOnly` and produces an exactly zero mesh delta.
- `shoulderBreadthRatio`, `biacromialRatio`, `abdominalDepthRatio` and
  `headHeightRatio` carry measurement-derived numbers but are NOT read by
  the generator; their deltas are zero. No claim of use is made.
- The shoulder girdle currently derives its width from `chestHalfWidth *
  girdleMass`, NOT from the measured biacromial breadth. Mapping those
  fields requires an owner-approved anatomical decision.
