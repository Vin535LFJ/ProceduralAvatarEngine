## 1. Synthetic construction

Two patches meet on a circular ring at y=0, radius 0.50 m, with outward
triangle winding. The upper patch is a cone of slope tan(fold); the true
cross-boundary dihedral is therefore the prescribed fold. Both normals are
read from the mesh's own triangles and index buffer.

## 4. Technical interpretation (not a decision)

- The legacy statistic changes with 360/segments on a perfect fold and is
  therefore not a stable estimator of the cross-boundary dihedral.
- The cross-boundary statistic reads the prescribed fold at every sampled
  segment count and density in this synthetic construction.
- Whether this faithfully expresses the original Geometry Mesh Contract
  semantic ('normal angle on both sides of a seam') remains an OWNER decision.
- The current v2 shared envelope is ONE continuous surface with no second
  component at the crop ring, so on the real mesh this experiment is
  NOT APPLICABLE — which is not PASS.
